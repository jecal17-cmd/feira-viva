Run your own market stall, merge fruits and Brazilian treats, and travel to Brazil's most famous markets, with no rush and no losing.

In Quitanda you run a street-market stall. Drag an item onto a matching one to merge them into something better: a lime becomes an orange, an orange becomes a banana, all the way up to an açaí bowl. Deliver what your customers ask for, earn coins and XP, and watch your quitanda (a small Brazilian produce shop) grow.

🧺 SATISFYING MERGES
• 13 item lines with 8 levels each, almost all from Brazil: fruits, fish, spices, sweets, grains and more
• Triple merge: drag an item across two matching neighbors and all three jump up two levels at once
• Baskets refill over time, even while the game is closed

🗺️ A TRIP ACROSS BRAZIL
• 12 real markets, from Ver-o-Peso in Belém to the Mercado Público in Porto Alegre
• Travel by bus or plane across the map of Brazil and discover each region's market
• The trail never ends: every lap brings more XP and more coins

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 THE MARKET CREW
• 28 characters to meet, including Seu Zé, Bia and Mingau the cat
• Every order you fill grows your friendship, with no limit

📖 RECIPES, MISSIONS AND DECOR
• A Recipe Book full of Brazilian dishes to discover
• Missions, special orders and a daily gift
• Upgrade your baskets and decorate your stall with an awning, string lights, festa junina flags and more

🌿 AT YOUR OWN PACE
• No losing and no failed levels: play for five minutes or a whole afternoon
• Calm music and soft sounds (you can turn them off)
• Available in English and Portuguese

The game is free and contains ads. Some ads are optional and give rewards, such as refilling your baskets or doubling an order's payment.

Quitanda is a Bentevi Games title, made in Brazil.
